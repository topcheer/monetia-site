FAQ

Questions, answered

Pricing, devices, how recognition works and where your data goes.

See all support questions →

How much does Moneta cost?

A one-time purchase on the App Store (US$1.99 in the US store; prices vary by region). No subscription, no account, no ads.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone (iOS 17+), iPad (iPadOS 17+) and Mac (macOS 14+, Mac Catalyst). One purchase covers all three, and the ledger syncs through your own iCloud.

How does voice booking work?

Say "Lunch 35" and it is booked. On-device speech recognition runs first, with AI audio understanding as a fallback. Microphone and Speech Recognition permissions are required.

What does receipt scanning extract?

OCR and AI pull the amount, merchant, date and category from a photo — up to 3 receipts at a time — and show a confidence so you can double-check before booking.

Which AI does it use, and is my data sent anywhere?

Gemini is built in through Firebase AI Logic, and you can connect any OpenAI-compatible endpoint instead (DeepSeek, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama). The ledger is local-first; only what you send to the AI is processed, and only when you ask.

Can I export or migrate my data?

Yes — CSV export and import anytime under Settings → Data; the files open in Excel or Numbers. 26 currencies with built-in rates work offline.
