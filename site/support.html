Moneta Support

Frequently asked questions and how to reach us.

Voice booking does not react

Open Settings → Moneta and allow Microphone and Speech Recognition. On iOS 26, choose Allow on the first-run prompts.

Receipt recognition fails

Make sure the amount area is sharp and well lit. If recognition still fails, type the amount manually or paste the email text into the AI chat to parse it.

iCloud sync is not working

Both devices must be signed in to the same Apple ID with iCloud Drive enabled. The first sync can take a few minutes.

How are exchange rates calculated?

26 common currencies ship with built-in reference rates and work offline. Currencies that are not listed are booked 1:1; the original amount is always kept.

Which AI models can I use?

Firebase AI (Gemini) is built in. In Settings you can connect any OpenAI-compatible endpoint — DeepSeek, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama and others.

How do I export my data?

Settings → Data → Export CSV opens in Excel or Numbers. You can also import CSV to migrate from another app.

Contact

If none of this helps, write to monetia@monetia.net — we answer within two business days.
