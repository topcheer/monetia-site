Moneta Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 31, 2026

1. General

Moneta (the "App") is developed by an independent developer ("we", "us"). Our core principle is local-first: your ledger data is stored on your device by default, and we do not operate any backend server that holds your records.

2. What we collect

  • Ledger data (stored locally): transaction amounts, categories, notes, merchants and receipt images are all kept in the app's on-device database (SwiftData) and, if you turn on sync, synced privately through your own iCloud to your other devices. This data is never sent to us.
  • AI processing (on demand, transmitted only when used): only when you use voice booking, receipt recognition or the AI assistant is the relevant text, audio or image sent to the AI provider you have configured (by default Firebase AI Logic / Google, or any OpenAI-compatible provider you enter yourself). We attach no account identifier to such requests and cannot link the content to you. Before you first use an AI feature, the App discloses this data flow in an in-app dialog and asks for your consent; if you decline, AI features stay unavailable while everything else keeps working. You can change this at any time under Settings → AI provider.
  • What we do not collect: no account registration, no advertising identifier (IDFA), no third-party tracking SDKs, no behavioural analytics.

3. Storage and security

  • Ledger data is stored with iOS Data Protection; iCloud sync uses Apple's end-to-end encrypted transport.
  • Receipt images are stored on the device only and can be deleted at any time when editing a record.

4. Third-party services

  • Firebase AI Logic / Google Gemini: processes the AI requests you initiate. Subject to the Google Privacy Policy.
  • OpenAI-compatible providers you configure yourself (for example DeepSeek, OpenRouter or Ollama): chosen and configured by you; their data handling follows the respective provider's policies.
  • Apple iCloud: used only if you enable sync; the data stays within your own iCloud account.

5. Permissions

  • Microphone / Speech Recognition: records only while you hold the record button. On-device transcription is tried first; audio is sent to the AI only if transcription fails.
  • Camera / Photos: only for taking or picking receipt images.
  • Notifications: only for the 80% / 100% budget alerts.

6. Your rights

  • Export: Settings → Data → Export CSV takes all of your data with you at any time.
  • Deletion: deleting the App deletes all data on the device; after uninstall we retain nothing, because we never collected it.
  • AI off: if you do not use AI features, no data ever leaves the device.

7. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will announce them in the App and update this page.

9. Contact

Privacy questions: monetia@monetia.net
